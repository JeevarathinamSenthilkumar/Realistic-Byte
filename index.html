About Us

Realistic Byte helps restaurants transform their menus into immersive Augmented Reality (AR) experiences. These experiences make every dish come to life and spark customers’ cravings before they even order.

We blend creativity, technology, and thoughtful design. This approach turns ordinary menus into interactive journeys that captivate attention, highlight your unique offerings, and inspire guests to explore and order more.

Our mission is clear: to elevate your restaurant’s presentation, enhance the dining experience, and provide a modern, memorable way for customers to connect with your brand. Ultimately, this approach boosts engagement and increases sales.

What Do We Offer

Our Solution: See it, Love it, Order it

Our service transforms your restaurant’s current menu presentation into an immersive dish preview. We convert your existing food photography into stunning, lifelike 3D models of every dish. Our technology then packages these assets into a custom-branded digital menu for your restaurant, accessible instantly by your customers.

Key Deliverables

  • Hyper-Realistic 3D Models: High-fidelity visualization showcasing the texture, color, and precise portion size of your food.
  • Custom Digital Menu Interface: A fully branded, intuitive, and easy-to-navigate front-end tailored to your restaurant's aesthetics.
  • Instant AR Visualization: Seamless, web-based AR technology that allows customers to view dishes on their table with no special app required.
